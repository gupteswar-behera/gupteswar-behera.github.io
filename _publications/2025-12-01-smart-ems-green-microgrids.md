**Abstract.** This paper presents a comparative techno-economic analysis of pumped hydro energy storage (PHES) and battery energy storage systems (BESS) for low operational cost scheduling in a solar rich microgrid. A one-month load profile of an academic campus is considered, where the load is met using solar photovoltaic (PV) generation, grid power, and an energy storage device (ESD). Both load and solar PV availability are forecasted using a long short-term memory (LSTM) model. An optimization-based dispatch that minimizes the total operational cost under the constraint of meeting demand at all times is proposed here. Results show that the PHES configuration achieves a final operational cost of 3.83 INR/kWh, 12.53% lower than the 4.31 INR/kWh for the BESS configuration.

**Keywords:** energy scheduling, pumped hydro energy storage, battery energy storage system, forecasting, energy systems, cost optimization.
