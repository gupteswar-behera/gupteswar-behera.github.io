**Abstract.** The disclosure relates to a smart energy management system for optimising energy use in a power network. An energy advisor module determines technical ratings for renewable energy components from energy consumption information. A forecasting module predicts energy demand and renewable generation as time series. An optimisation module determines an optimal power flow schedule among several energy sources, at least one energy storage device and loads, by minimising operating cost or carbon emissions; it comprises a source optimisation module for generation, storage operation and grid interaction, and a demand optimisation module that assigns priority levels and issues load curtailment or supply commands. A communication interface sends commands and provides operational data.
