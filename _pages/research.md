Research
------

My research focuses on optimizing multi-source energy management within microgrids to minimize operational costs and carbon emissions while ensuring reliable load fulfillment. Specifically, I model the integration of renewable generation (solar photovoltaic systems), conventional power sources (utility grids and diesel generators), and diverse energy storage technologies-including batteries, pumped hydro, compressed air, and hydrogen storage. To validate these frameworks, I test them against empirical data-incorporating load profiles, solar irradiance, ambient temperature, and Indian Energy Exchange (IEX) market prices-sourced from a utility-scale solar-plus-storage plant in Gujarat.

Comparing energy storage technologies
------
Solar generation peaks at midday while electricity demand and grid tariffs peak in the evening; energy storage is essential for shifting power across these intervals. However, storage technologies vary significantly in round-trip efficiency, usable capacity, and operating costs. In my [IEEE STPEC 2025 paper](/publications/), I developed an optimization-based energy management system (EMS) to schedule a campus microgrid configured with either pumped hydro or battery storage. The pumped hydro configuration achieved a final operating cost of **3.83 INR/kWh, outperforming the battery setup (4.31 INR/kWh) by 12.53%**.

I am now expanding this comparative analysis to evaluate four distinct storage technologies-battery, pumped hydro, compressed air, and hydrogen fuel cells-using standardized nameplate ratings and incorporating high-fidelity models of each technology's unique operational characteristics.
