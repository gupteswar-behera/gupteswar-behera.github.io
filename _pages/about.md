About me
------

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the **Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar (IITGN)**, working in the **Smart Power Electronics Laboratory (SPEL)**.

My research is on **energy management systems (EMS) for solar-rich microgrids with energy storage**. I develop optimization models (mixed-integer linear and nonlinear programming) and rule-based controllers that schedule solar generation, grid power, and storage so that demand is met at the lowest operating cost, and test them on real data: the IIT Gandhinagar campus load and solar, Indian Energy Exchange prices, and a year of measurements from a utility-scale solar and battery plant in Gujarat. Application and analysis of various energy storage technologies, such as batteries, pumped hydro, compressed air, and hydrogen storage, within the same microgrid; modeling their part-load efficiency; and extending the work to carbon-aware dispatch and reinforcement learning are also part of my work.
