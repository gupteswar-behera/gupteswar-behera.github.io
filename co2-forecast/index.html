Hourly CO₂ intensity of the Indian grid

Grid CO₂ intensity is the mass of CO₂ emitted for each megawatt-hour of electricity generated in India, hour by hour; it is lowest around midday, when solar generation is high, and highest at night, when coal carries most of the load. Knowing it a day or two ahead lets flexible use, such as charging a battery, move to the cleanest hours.

Notes

  • The same-day forecast of a day is issued at 00:00 of that day; the day-ahead forecast is issued at 00:00 of the day before. Each forecast uses only data up to its issue time.
  • Errors are the mean absolute percentage error (MAPE) and the mean absolute error (MAE) in kg/MWh, over every measured hour of the days shown. The baseline, “yesterday repeated”, takes the last measured day as the forecast.
  • Forecasts for the days before the daily updates began were computed afterwards in the same way, using only data up to each issue time. Since then, each day's forecast is saved as published and never changed.

Data sources

Generation data: The CSEP Electricity and Carbon Tracker (carbontracker.in). Emission factors: Central Electricity Authority, CO₂ Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector, version 22.0.
